## Game Rules and Tips for How to Play
There are a few rules in the game that is easy to learn or acquire:
    1. A dinosour will appear and will be waking and cactus will come at the direction to the dinosour. Your mission is to jump over the cactuses. If the dinosour touches cactus, the game will over and your total score is shown.

    2.  
        i. Press "space" or "up arrow" to jump
        ii. Press "down arrow" to sneak
        iii. Hold "W" to activate "cactus skipping" mode (would remain 20 sec)
                {Note: the power will only skip cactus, but not the dragons}
                [it will only work when you will get "cactus skipping orb"]
        iv. Hold "S" to activate "end of the dragon" mode
                {Note: the power will destroy the upcoming 30 dragons}
        v. At night, the dragon can't see the cactus or dragons, so the torch is needed.
           the torch will appear like orbs and will be activated by holding "A" (would remain for 60 sec)
